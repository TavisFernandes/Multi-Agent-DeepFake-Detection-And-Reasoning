Interactive Information Cards

The four feature cards and the four analysis cards should not only have hover animations.

Each card must be clickable.

When clicked, a premium animated modal should appear in the centre of the screen.

The modal should feel like Apple's product pages, Linear, or Notion AI.

Do not use a plain dialog box.

Instead use:

Glassmorphism
Frosted blur background
Soft shadows
Animated entrance
Rounded corners
Gradient borders
Smooth scaling animation
Background blur
Click outside to close
ESC key closes modal

Animation:

Fade in
Scale from 95% → 100%

CARD 1
Multiple Sources of Evidence

Modal Title

Looking at More Than One Clue

Introduction

VERITAS doesn't rely on just one signal to decide whether something is real or fake. Instead, it examines several different types of evidence before reaching a conclusion.

How it Works

When you upload content, VERITAS looks at:

• The visual appearance of images and videos

• Movement between video frames

• Audio quality and speech characteristics

• Information available from trusted online sources

Each source provides a different piece of the puzzle.

By combining them, VERITAS can make a more balanced judgement.

Why This Matters

Some fake content looks convincing visually but contains unusual audio.

Others have realistic audio but inconsistent movement.

Looking at multiple clues helps reduce mistakes.

Example

Imagine a video where:

The person's face looks realistic.

The voice sounds artificial.

The original source cannot be verified.

Taken together, these signs make the content more suspicious than any one clue alone.

Privacy Note

Your uploaded content is analysed only to generate the requested result.

CARD 2
Everything Considered Together

Modal Title

One Clear Answer, Not Four Separate Opinions

Introduction

Instead of showing separate results for each type of analysis, VERITAS combines all findings into one easy-to-understand explanation.

How it Works

Different analyses may sometimes disagree.

For example,

Visual inspection may suggest the content appears authentic.

Audio analysis may detect unusual speech patterns.

Context verification may find conflicting information online.

VERITAS weighs these observations together before producing a final explanation.

Why This Matters

People shouldn't need to understand technical reports.

VERITAS simplifies everything into one final conclusion with supporting reasoning.

Example

Rather than saying:

Visual: OK

Audio: Suspicious

Context: Unknown

VERITAS explains:

"While the visuals appear natural, unusual audio characteristics and missing supporting context reduce confidence in the authenticity of this content."

CARD 3
Easy-to-Understand Explanations

Modal Title

Clear Answers Without Technical Jargon

Introduction

AI tools often produce confusing scores and unfamiliar terminology.

VERITAS explains its findings in plain language.

How it Works

Every result includes:

What was observed

Why it matters

How those observations influenced the conclusion

Suggestions for interpreting the result

Why This Matters

People should understand why a conclusion was reached instead of simply accepting an automated decision.

Example

Instead of:

"Inference confidence = 0.91"

VERITAS says:

"We found several visual and audio inconsistencies that are commonly associated with AI-generated media."

CARD 4
Honest About Uncertainty

Modal Title

Sometimes the Answer Isn't Certain

Introduction

No automated system can guarantee perfect accuracy.

VERITAS communicates uncertainty honestly.

How it Works

Instead of pretending every answer is certain, VERITAS uses simple confidence levels.

High Confidence

Moderate Confidence

Low Confidence

These indicate how strongly the available evidence supports the conclusion.

Why This Matters

Being transparent helps users make informed decisions rather than relying blindly on automation.

Example

A heavily compressed video with poor audio may not contain enough information for a confident decision.

In those situations, VERITAS clearly explains why the result is less certain.

ANALYSIS CARD POPUPS

Each of the four animated analysis cards should also open a modal with more information.

Visual Analysis

Modal Title

Visual Analysis

Hero Illustration

Animated face mesh or image grid.

Introduction

VERITAS carefully examines images and individual video frames for visual clues that may indicate digital manipulation.

It looks for subtle inconsistencies that are often difficult to notice with the naked eye.

Checks Performed

• Facial proportions and alignment

• Lighting and shadows

• Skin texture consistency

• Reflections

• Blending around edges

• Image quality variations

• Background consistency

Why It Matters

Many manipulated images contain tiny imperfections that become noticeable when examined closely.

Example

An image may appear realistic at first glance but contain inconsistent lighting around the face or blurred edges that suggest digital editing.

Motion Analysis

Modal Title

Motion Analysis

Hero Illustration

Animated timeline with moving frames.

Introduction

Videos are made of many individual frames.

VERITAS studies how movement changes across those frames.

Checks Performed

• Natural facial movement

• Lip synchronisation

• Eye blinking

• Head movement

• Motion smoothness

• Object consistency

• Scene transitions

Why It Matters

AI-generated videos sometimes produce small visual glitches that appear only while the video is moving.

Example

A person's face may briefly flicker or change shape between frames even though the overall video appears realistic.

Audio Analysis

Modal Title

Audio Analysis

Hero Illustration

Animated waveform.

Introduction

VERITAS analyses speech and sound for signs that the audio may have been generated, cloned, or modified.

Checks Performed

• Voice consistency

• Speech rhythm

• Background sounds

• Unusual distortions

• Abrupt transitions

• Overall audio quality

Why It Matters

Synthetic voices often contain subtle patterns that differ from naturally recorded speech.

Example

A voice may sound convincing but contain repeating background noise or unusually consistent pronunciation.

Context Verification

Modal Title

Context Verification

Hero Illustration

Animated network graph.

Introduction

VERITAS looks beyond the media itself.

It compares available information with trusted online sources to determine whether the content matches what it claims to be.

Checks Performed

• Source credibility

• Publication information

• Metadata consistency

• Related reports

• Publicly available references

• Timeline consistency

Why It Matters

Even authentic media can be misleading if shared with false claims or taken out of context.

Example

A genuine photograph from several years ago may be reposted during a current event with a misleading description.
