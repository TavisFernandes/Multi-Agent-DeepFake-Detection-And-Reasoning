Page Transition Animations (Landing Page ↔ Launch Console)

The transition between the Landing Page and the Launch Console should feel cinematic and premium. It should not instantly switch pages or simply fade.

Launch Console Transition

When the user clicks the "Launch Console" button:

Animate the entire landing page into the console instead of performing an abrupt route change.
Use Framer Motion for page transitions.
Duration: 800–1200ms.
The transition should feel fluid, elegant, and intentional.
Suggested Animation Sequence
The Launch Console button subtly expands and emits a soft cyan/purple glow.
The background neural network and particles accelerate slightly.
The hero content smoothly fades upward while becoming slightly blurred.
A translucent glass layer expands from the centre of the screen, as if opening a portal into the workspace.
The camera performs a gentle zoom-in (approximately 5–8%).
The screen briefly fills with a moving gradient or animated light sweep.
The console interface slides into view while fading from 0% to 100% opacity.
The chat interface, upload panel, and navigation animate in sequentially with a slight stagger (80–120ms delay between elements).

The result should feel like the user is entering an intelligent workspace, not simply navigating to another page.

Return to Home Transition

Inside the console, include a Back to Home button in the navigation/header.

When clicked:

Reverse the experience with the same level of polish.
Do not instantly navigate back.
Suggested Animation Sequence
The console slightly zooms out.
The chat messages softly fade and blur.
Upload cards and panels slide away gracefully.
The background transitions into the animated landing page.
The hero title fades back in with its original entrance animation.
The navigation bar reappears smoothly.
The particle background slows back to its normal motion.

The transition should make it feel like the user is leaving the analysis workspace and returning to the public website.

Transition Requirements
Use Framer Motion's AnimatePresence for route transitions.
Preserve smooth scrolling and avoid white flashes during navigation.
Keep the animated background persistent between pages where possible for visual continuity.
Maintain 60 FPS animations.
Respect the user's prefers-reduced-motion setting by providing a simplified fade transition.
The transitions should feel comparable to premium experiences seen in Apple, Linear, Arc Browser, Vercel, and Notion, prioritising elegance over excessive effects.
